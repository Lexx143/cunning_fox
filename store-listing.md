```
A sneaky fox stole the pie and is running to its den! Help the animal detective squad — badger, otter, hedgehog and raccoon — catch the thief before it hides.

HOW TO PLAY
• Roll the dice: "eyes" flip suspect cards, "paws" give you steps across the forest meadow

• Compare clues with the traits of 16 fox suspects and deduce the thief!
• But be careful: every mistake lets the fox run closer to its den

FEATURES
• Co-op play for 1–4 players on one screen
• Built-in tutorial right in the first game — even preschoolers get it
• Develops logic, deduction and attention
• Cute hand-drawn art and gentle music
• Fully offline — play anywhere
• No ads, no data collection
• English and Russian languages

Save the pie — become the best detective in the forest!
```
